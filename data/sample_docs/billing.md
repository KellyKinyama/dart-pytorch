# Billing

Our billing cycle runs monthly, on the day you originally subscribed. Invoices
are sent to the primary email on the account 48 hours before the charge.

Your subscription can be canceled at any time from Account → Billing. The
cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you
retain full access until then. Refund requests are honored within 30 days of
the original charge if you have not yet used more than 10% of your monthly
quota.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google
Pay. We do not currently accept PayPal or cryptocurrency. Failed payments
retry twice over five days; if the retries also fail your account moves to a
grace-period tier with reduced quotas.

## Non-profit discounts

Registered 501(c)(3) organizations receive a 50% discount on the Pro plan.
Contact sales@example.com with a copy of your determination letter to apply.
