Our support hours are Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm Pacific time. We are closed on major US holidays.
To reset your password, click the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in page and follow the emailed instructions.
Your subscription can be canceled at any time from the Account -> Billing page. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not currently accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.
All plans include a 14-day free trial. You will not be charged until the trial ends and can cancel at any time before then without a fee.
Refund requests are honored within 30 days of the original charge if you have not yet used more than 10% of your monthly quota.
Multi-factor authentication is available via authenticator apps (TOTP), SMS, or hardware security keys (WebAuthn/FIDO2).
Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Encryption keys are rotated every 90 days.
We are SOC 2 Type II certified and comply with GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA for eligible customers on our Enterprise plan.
The mobile app is available on the App Store for iOS 15 and later and on Google Play for Android 9 (Pie) and later.
API requests are rate-limited to 60 requests per minute for the Free plan, 600 for Pro, and 6000 for Enterprise.
API keys can be created and revoked from the Developer Console under Settings -> API Access.
Uploaded files may be up to 100 MB each on the Free plan, 2 GB on Pro, and 20 GB on Enterprise.
We retain backups for 30 days on the Free plan and 90 days on Pro and Enterprise.
Support tickets can be opened by email at support@example.com or through the in-app help widget. Enterprise plans include a dedicated Slack channel.
Live phone support is available for Enterprise customers only, at +1-800-555-0199, 24 hours a day.
Your team can invite additional members from the Team Settings page. The Pro plan includes up to 10 seats; the Enterprise plan is unlimited.
Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML or OIDC is available on the Enterprise plan and requires configuration by your IT administrator.
We offer a free Non-Profit discount of 50% for registered 501(c)(3) organizations. Contact sales to apply.
The status page at status.example.com reports live uptime, ongoing incidents, and scheduled maintenance for every region.
